# Getting help with AppJars

AppJars has three channels. Picking the right one is the fastest way to get an answer.

## 1. Discussions — questions and conversation

**https://github.com/orgs/AppJars/discussions**

Open to everyone, free users included. This is the place for:

- "Is X supported?"
- "Are you planning to do Y?"
- "How do people usually approach Z with this AppJar?"
- Ideas for new AppJars
- Showing what you have built

Discussions are a community forum. Other users answer, and so do we when we can add something. If a discussion turns out to be a reproducible defect or a concrete feature proposal, open an issue for it.

## 2. Issues — bugs and feature requests

**https://github.com/AppJars/appjars/issues**

Open to everyone. Two kinds of report:

- **Bug report** — an AppJar does not behave as documented.
- **Feature request** — something you would like an AppJar to do.

Fixing defects is part of the product. It is not a paid service and it does not require a subscription. Free and paid users report in the same tracker, under the same rules.

A feature request is a record of your need, not a commitment from us. We decide what we build.

**What an issue is not:** it is not a channel for help with your own application. "It does not build in my project", "how do I configure this for my setup", "can you look at my code" — those belong in the support portal (see below), or in Discussions if you are happy to ask in public and wait for whoever answers.

## 3. Customer portal — help with your application

**https://customers.flowingcode.com** — included with Startup and Enterprise subscriptions.

The portal runs on the Flowing Code domain because Flowing Code S.A. develops AppJars and delivers its support. It is the right place, even though the address does not say `appjars.com`.

It gives you two things:

- **Human assistance on your application** — integrating an AppJar, installing it, configuring it, understanding how to use it, reviewing your approach. Delivered privately, against the support hours in your subscription.
- **An AI assistant**, with access included in both the Startup and Enterprise tiers. Fastest route for questions about configuration, usage and documentation, and it does not consume support hours.

If you use AppJars for free and need this kind of help, we may be able to offer it as paid assistance — ask in Discussions or contact us through the website.

## Issue escalation (Enterprise)

Enterprise subscribers can escalate a bug: request that a specific public issue be moved to the front of the queue.

- Escalation is requested **through the support portal**, never on GitHub. Opening an escalation ticket is the only way to escalate. Asking in a comment on an issue does nothing.
- Escalation applies to **bug reports only**. Feature requests are not eligible.
- Each license carries **one concurrent escalation**. The slot stays occupied until we close the escalation ticket.
- The public effect is the **`Prioritized`** label on the issue. The label never identifies who escalated it.
- Escalation buys queue position. It carries **no service level, no committed timeframe, and no guarantee that the bug will be fixed**. We may propose a workaround instead. If we decide not to fix it, we tell you why and close the escalation ticket, which frees your slot.
- An escalation is resolved when the fix is **published in a release** and you have been able to verify it.
- If an issue already carries `Prioritized`, escalating it again is not necessary and does not add anything. We will tell you it is already escalated and close the ticket without consuming your slot.

This is why the label is public: so you can see what is already at the front of the queue before spending your escalation on it.

## Security vulnerabilities

Do not report suspected vulnerabilities as ordinary issues or in Discussions. Follow the process at **https://www.appjars.com/security/**.

## Response expectations

Issues and Discussions are monitored, but neither carries a response commitment. The only channel with contractual response terms is the support portal, under the terms of your subscription: https://www.appjars.com/legal/
